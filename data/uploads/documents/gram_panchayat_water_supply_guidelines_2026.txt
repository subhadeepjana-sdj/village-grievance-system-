GRAM PANCHAYAT CHANDANPUR OFFICIAL WATER SUPPLY CITIZEN CHARTER 2026

1. Service Timings: Drinking water is supplied twice daily from 6:00 AM to 9:00 AM and 5:00 PM to 7:00 PM.
2. Complaint Resolution SLA: Drinking water contamination or supply breakdown complaints must be inspected within 24 hours and resolved within 48 hours by the Village Water Department.
3. Pipeline Leaks: Citizens can report major main-line leaks directly to Officer Vikram (+91 98765 11111).
4. Water Testing: Gram Panchayat conducts bi-weekly water quality testing at Ward 1, Ward 2, Ward 3, and Ward 4 public taps.

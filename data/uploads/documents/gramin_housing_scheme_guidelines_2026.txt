GRAMIN HOUSING SCHEME (PMAY-G) VILLAGE IMPLEMENTATION GUIDELINES 2026

1. Financial Assistance: Financial subsidy of Rs 1,20,000 is provided for plain areas and Rs 1,30,000 for hilly areas for pucca house construction.
2. Eligibility: Homeless households and families living in zero, one, or two-room kucha houses as per SECC 2011 data.
3. Application Procedure: Eligible citizens must register at the Gram Panchayat office with Aadhaar card, Job Card number, and bank account details.
4. Installments: Fund transfer is disbursed in 3 direct bank transfer installments linked to construction milestone geotagging.

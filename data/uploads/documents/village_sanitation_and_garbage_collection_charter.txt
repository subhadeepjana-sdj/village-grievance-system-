GRAM PANCHAYAT CHANDANPUR SANITATION & WASTE MANAGEMENT RULES

1. Waste Collection Schedule: Door-to-door waste collection operates daily between 7:00 AM and 10:00 AM in all wards.
2. Segregation: Citizens must separate wet organic waste (green bin) from dry recyclable waste (blue bin).
3. Overflow Bins SLA: Complaints regarding overflowing public waste bins will be attended to within 12 hours of lodging.
